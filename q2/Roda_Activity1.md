Activity 1 – Inheritance

1. What is/are the parent class/es of the Programmer class in the video?
The parent class of Programmer is Employee, and its superclass is Person.

2. If you were to add a new role called Intern, where would you place it in the inheritance hierarchy? Why?
I would place Intern under Employee, alongside Programmer, Manager, and Cleaner, because an intern is also a type of employee and can inherit common properties and methods from the Employee and Person classes.
